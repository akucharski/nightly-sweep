How your keys are kept

  • Each key is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it's saved, and sealed to your account and its name.
  • After saving, a key is never shown again, not even to you. You'll see only its last four characters.
  • Keys are used only for sweeps, model lists and connection tests that you start, and are sent only to the service they belong to.
  • Administrators can't read your keys from the dashboard. Remove a key here at any time; removing your account removes them all.
